## New Features: 

- ### Lunar rituals 

- **Moon Altar** -  Allows you to perform various rituals under the moonlight to obtain various items. 

- **Moon Pedestal** - It is an integral part of lunar rituals and is used to hold various objects. 

- **Moon Pebbles** - Moon pebbles that once fell from the sky. They can be found in forests and used to craft various items. 

- **Moonstone** - Decorative block that crafted from moon pebbles and used to create various items. 

- **Moon Dust** - A powder imbued with the power of the moon. Used for various rituals. 

- **Moon Gold** - Key material obtained through rituals. 

- ### Lunar accessories 

- **Moon Ring with Emerald** - Increases attack speed by 5% and attack range by 1 block. 

- **Moon Ring with Diamond** - Increases running speed by 10%. 

- **Moon Oculars** - They allow you to see better in the dark, i.e. provide night vision. 

- **White Moon Amulet** - Allows you to prevent fall damage once per minute. 

- **Lunar Magnet Amulet** - Allows you to attract objects Allows you to attract objects within a 7-block radius. This ability can be toggled on and off, and can also be used by pressing a special button. 

- ### New Weapons 

- All types of bows have been added. 

## Fixes and improvements: 

- Full compatibility with Better Combat has been added. 

- Some improvements and fixes at the code level.
